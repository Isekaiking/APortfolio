About

Who is behind the frame?

Isekai King is a motion graphic designer and video editor working across corporate, brand, healthcare, political, and social content — 3+ years spent moving between very different rooms with the same instinct: make it move well.

The work runs from a logo animation that a client's leadership praised on sight, to 50+ campaign reels cut inside a live political cycle, to a 100-layer AI advertisement built to look like it came out of a product studio, not a freelancer's laptop. Along the way he's also secured and delivered brand deals and sponsorships — including manhwa animation and storytelling video integrations — managing the full creative execution from brief to final delivery.

Currently based in Lucknow, India — building a freelance practice alongside a growing YouTube and Instagram audience of over 176K followers.
